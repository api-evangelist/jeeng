# Jeeng

> Jeeng (formerly PowerInbox) is a multichannel monetization and audience-engagement platform for digital publishers, spanning email newsletters, the web, push notifications, and newsreader apps across 150M+ opt-in subscribers. Jeeng publishes no public developer API; publisher integration is handled through merge tags, per-newsletter ad tags, cs_email tracking parameters, and direct Google Ad Manager (GAM) integration, with campaigns and reporting managed through the PowerInbox Portal.

## Products

- [Jeeng AdFill](https://www.jeeng.com/): Automatically fills vacant ad inventory with monetized content.
- [Jeeng AdServe](https://www.jeeng.com/): Extends direct ad campaigns into email through existing ad servers.
- [Jeeng AdMarket](https://www.jeeng.com/): Connects advertisers with 150M+ unique opt-in subscribers in email, on the web, and in push.
- [Jeeng Renderer](https://www.jeeng.com/): Streamlines Google Ad Manager ad-ops workflows.
